# 1701 · Who am I?

**Topic:** Users, groups, sessions, logs & cron · **Difficulty:** ★☆☆☆☆ · **Commands:** whoami, id, groups

Print, one per line:

1. your login name (`whoami`)
2. your numeric UID (`id -u`)
3. the **name** of your primary group (`id -gn`)
4. the names of all your groups (`groups` or `id -Gn`)
5. the full `id` output

# 2012 · Lines 5 to 9

**Topic:** Exam tasks with the manual · **Difficulty:** ★★☆☆☆ · **Commands:** head, tail

`registro.txt` has between 12 and 20 lines. Print **only its lines 5 to 9**, both included.

Example: for the lines `l1 l2 ... l12` the output is `l5 l6 l7 l8 l9`, one per line.

Two commands in a pipe do it (look at the `-n` forms of `head` and `tail`; `+N` and `-N` mean different things).
